# Headphones

## Choosing headphones
Decide on use first: commuting and travel favour active noise cancelling (ANC) and 30 hour battery; the gym favours water resistant earbuds; music mixing favours wired, flat-sounding studio headphones. Over-ear designs are usually more comfortable for long sessions.

## Wireless features that matter
Bluetooth multipoint lets you connect to a phone and laptop at once. Check codec support if you care about quality. ANC quality varies a lot by price: budget ANC is good for engine and fan noise, premium ANC is better at voices.
