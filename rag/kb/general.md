# General buying advice

## Reading reviews
A high rating with few reviews is less reliable than a slightly lower rating with thousands. Check the share of low-star reviews and what they complain about: repeated complaints about the same defect are a red flag.

## Price and value
The cheapest option often costs more over time if it fails or feels slow. Compare price against the one or two features you actually need, and ignore extras you will not use. Check the return policy and warranty before buying.
