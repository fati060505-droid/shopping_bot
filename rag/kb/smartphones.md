# Smartphones

## Reading phone specs
For daily use 6 to 8GB RAM and 128GB storage is enough; pick 256GB if you shoot lots of video. A 5000mAh battery usually gives a full day or more. 90Hz or 120Hz screens feel smoother. Camera megapixels matter less than sensor size and software processing, so rely on reviews and sample photos.

## Software support
Check how many years of security updates the maker promises. Two extra years of updates is often worth paying slightly more for. 5G only helps if your carrier and area support it.
