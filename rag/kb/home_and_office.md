# Home and office

## Blenders
Wattage is a rough guide: 300 to 600W suits soft smoothies, 1000W and above handles ice, frozen fruit and nut butters. Look at jar size, dishwasher-safe parts and noise level in reviews.

## Office chairs
For long sessions look for adjustable lumbar support, adjustable armrests, seat depth adjustment and a breathable mesh back. Warranty length is a good proxy for build quality.

## Backpacks
Pick the size by use: 20L for commuting, 30L for work plus gym, 40L and above for travel. Padded laptop sleeve, water resistance and strap padding are the features that reviewers mention most often.
