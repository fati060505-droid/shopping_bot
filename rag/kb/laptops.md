# Laptops

## What to check when buying a laptop
Match RAM to the job: 8GB is the floor for browsing, 16GB is the sensible default for work and multitasking, 32GB only for heavy editing or development. Prefer an SSD of at least 512GB. Check the weight and the battery claim; real battery life is usually 60 to 70 percent of the advertised figure.

## Gaming and creative laptops
A dedicated GPU matters for gaming, video editing and 3D work. Look at the GPU model first, then the screen refresh rate (120Hz or higher for gaming). Expect heavier bodies, louder fans and shorter battery life. Cooling quality shows up in reviews, so read the comments on thermals.

## Student and budget laptops
Avoid 4GB RAM and 64GB or 128GB eMMC storage; they feel slow within a year. A Ryzen 5 or Core i5 with 8 to 16GB is the best value tier.
